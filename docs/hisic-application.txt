企业/团队简介 / Company/Team profile
------------------------------------------------------------------------
AgriLink is an AgriTech startup founded to modernize the agricultural
value chain in developing markets, starting with small and medium-scale
farmers in Angola who are currently underserved by traditional
distribution and information channels. The team combines backend
engineering (Python/FastAPI), full-stack web development
(React/TypeScript), and mobile development (React Native/Expo), applied
directly to Angola's agricultural sector.

AgriLink is built as a production-grade platform, not a prototype: a
MySQL-backed relational data model spanning countries, regions, crops,
farms, products, orders, deliveries, IoT sensor data, and satellite
soil observations, architected from day one for multi-market
expansion. The platform already ships production-grade account
infrastructure (email verification, Google/Facebook sign-in) and
country-specific payment rails for all four target markets — including
Alipay, WeChat Pay, and UnionPay for China. Current focus is Angola,
where the marketplace, IoT monitoring, and satellite soil-diagnostics
modules are being validated with early farmer users before regional
scale-up.

The team's mission is to close the information and market-access gap
that keeps small farmers earning far below the real value of their
produce — giving them practical, low-cost tools instead of theoretical
promises. Our initial focus spans four markets: Angola (home base and
current pilot market), the Republic of the Congo and the Democratic
Republic of the Congo (adjacent African markets sharing similar
smallholder farming structures and market-access barriers), and China
(both a technology/hardware partner market for sensor-kit sourcing and
manufacturing, and a direct user market — Chinese farmers, buyers, and
distributors can register and use AgriLink themselves). The product
already ships with a working interface in four languages — Portuguese,
English, French, and Chinese — across both the web and mobile apps.

We are seeking innovation coaching, policy guidance, market matching,
talent, and financing support to execute this four-market rollout.

核心技术及产品简介 / Core technology and product
------------------------------------------------------------------------
AgriLink's product is a marketplace + logistics + IoT + satellite +
data-forecasting platform delivered as a web app (React/Vite/TypeScript),
a mobile app (React Native/Expo), and a FastAPI (Python) backend on
MySQL.

Four integrated layers:

1. Marketplace and logistics — farmers list products directly; buyers
   (consumers, distributors, food businesses) purchase without
   intermediaries; a dedicated transporter role handles last-mile
   delivery; buyers, farmers, and transporters message each other
   in-app. Checkout supports the payment methods people actually use
   in each market — Multicaixa Express and Unitel Money (Angola),
   Alipay, WeChat Pay, and UnionPay (China), Airtel Money and MTN
   Mobile Money (Republic of Congo), M-Pesa and Airtel Money (DRC),
   plus PayPal and Orange Money across markets — with a transparent
   10% platform commission on both farmer sales and transporter
   deliveries, and live earnings/withdrawal-balance dashboards for
   both roles.

2. IoT monitoring — low-cost sensors (soil moisture, temperature,
   water level, humidity) report per-farm readings, giving farmers
   real-time field visibility without manual inspection.

3. Satellite soil diagnostics — farmers draw their farm boundary on an
   interactive map (auto-centered on their current location); the
   backend runs that polygon through Google Earth Engine against
   Landsat satellite imagery to compute real soil indices (moisture,
   vegetation health, salinity) and turns them into plain-language
   recommendations — irrigate or not, plant now or wait, treat the
   soil — so farmers get actionable guidance even before installing
   any physical sensor.

4. Real data-driven forecasting — price forecasting uses an XGBoost
   model trained on real historical price data, real weather (via the
   Open-Meteo API: historical, live forecast, and climatological
   averages), and logged harvest timing, with recursive day-ahead
   prediction and confidence bands. Demand forecasting uses a Ridge
   regression model trained on real order volume with weekly
   seasonality. Both models report "insufficient data" rather than
   fabricating a forecast when real history is too thin — every number
   stays backend-verifiable rather than AI-washed.

- Native multi-language interface — the web app and mobile app both
  ship with a language switcher (Português, English, Français, 中文)
  covering the full user journey: navigation, marketplace browsing,
  registration, login, the farmer dashboard, and the admin panel. This
  directly addresses ease of entry into the Chinese market.

The data model already supports multi-country/multi-region operation,
so forecasting, marketplace, and payment logic generalize beyond
Angola without rearchitecture.

项目简介 / Project description
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Problem: Small and medium-scale farmers in Angola, the Republic of the
Congo, and the Democratic Republic of the Congo face shared structural
barriers — no direct market access, heavy dependence on intermediaries
who capture most of the margin, no real-time field data, post-harvest
waste, and no affordable technology. Farmers sell below fair value and
lose income and production they can't afford to lose.

Solution: AgriLink combines (1) a digital marketplace connecting
farmers directly to buyers, distributors, and food businesses, with a
dedicated transporter role for last-mile delivery; (2) low-cost IoT
sensors monitoring soil moisture, temperature, water level, and
humidity, plus satellite-based soil diagnostics via Google Earth
Engine for farmers without sensors installed yet; (3) a data-driven
decision layer turning sensor, satellite, weather, price-history, and
order data into price/demand forecasts and plain-language farming
recommendations; and (4) local payment rails with a transparent
commission model so farmers and transporters actually get paid —
designed to work across all three farming markets from a shared
platform.

How it works: sensors and satellite imagery stream field data to the
platform; the backend combines this with real weather data and
transaction history to generate forecasts and recommendations; farmers
act on them and sell directly through the marketplace; transporters
handle delivery and earn their own share; everyone gets paid through
the payment method already common in their market — one connected
loop from field to sale to payment, replicable farm-by-farm and
country-by-country.

Technology: Python/FastAPI backend, MySQL database, React/TypeScript
web app, React Native/Expo mobile app, XGBoost and Ridge regression
forecasting models, Open-Meteo weather API, Google Earth Engine
satellite analysis, and PayPal plus regional mobile-money
integrations. Full interface localization in Portuguese, English,
French, and Chinese.

Business model: a transparent 10% commission on both farmer sales and
transporter deliveries — live in the product today, with earnings and
withdrawal-balance dashboards for both roles — plus premium
subscriptions for advanced analytics, sale of low-cost sensor kits,
and agricultural data services for cooperatives, distributors, and
food businesses.

Market: initial rollout across four markets — Angola (home base and
current pilot), the Republic of the Congo and the Democratic Republic
of the Congo (adjacent African markets sharing the same smallholder
structure and market-access barriers) for the farmer/marketplace side,
and China, both as a technology/hardware and investment partner market
for sensor-kit sourcing/manufacturing AND as a direct target market for
farmers, cooperatives, and buyers using the platform themselves. The
data model already separates Country/Region/Crop entities, so operating
across all four markets is a configuration exercise, not a rebuild.

Impact: higher farmer income by removing intermediary markup, less
post-harvest waste, higher productivity from real-time field data, and
improved food security across all three farming markets.

项目亮点 / Project highlights
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Working product, not a concept deck — a running backend, web app, and
  native mobile app with real APIs for auth, farms, products, orders,
  deliveries, payments, sensors, satellite analysis, and market
  forecasting.

- Verifiable AI, not AI-washing — forecasting models train only on real
  price, weather, and order data, and honestly return "insufficient
  data" instead of inventing a number when history is thin.

- Recommendations without waiting for hardware — satellite soil
  diagnostics (Google Earth Engine) give farmers real irrigation and
  planting guidance from day one, before a single IoT sensor is
  installed on their farm.

- Real multi-country payments, not a slide — checkout already supports
  Alipay, WeChat Pay, and UnionPay for China alongside Multicaixa
  Express, Unitel Money, Airtel Money, MTN Mobile Money, M-Pesa,
  Orange Money, and PayPal — a concrete, working demonstration of
  cross-border readiness, not a roadmap promise.

- Four-in-one integration — marketplace + logistics, IoT + satellite
  field data, analytics, and localized payments in a single
  farm-to-sale-to-payment loop, where most competitors offer only one
  piece.

- Built for four-market operation in four languages from day one —
  Angola, Republic of the Congo, DRC, and China as a full user market
  (not just a supplier), with the web and mobile apps already
  localized in Portuguese, English, French, and Chinese — judges can
  switch the interface language live during a demo.

- Direct structural impact — removes the intermediary layer that
  currently captures most of a smallholder's margin.

- Accessible hardware — sensor kits priced for smallholder budgets, not
  enterprise precision-agriculture systems.

- Diversified monetization — transaction commissions, subscriptions,
  hardware sales, and data services, not a single revenue line.
